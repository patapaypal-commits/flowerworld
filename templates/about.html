our story

Made slowly, in small batches.

Flowerworld is a small studio making seasonal dried-flower arrangements, wreaths, and botanical home goods. Everything is hand-arranged in our studio using flowers grown locally and dried naturally.

A studio, not a factory

We started in a small studio with a simple belief: that a home should feel like the season it's in. Every wreath, every bunch, every arrangement is made by hand, one at a time, using dried botanicals we've grown, gathered, or sourced from local growers.

Nothing is mass-produced. Nothing is plastic. What you receive is a quiet, natural thing that will last for months — and then, when the time comes, return to the earth.
